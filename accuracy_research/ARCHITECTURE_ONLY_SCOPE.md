# Architecture-Only Research Scope

This active research tree uses no pretrained weights and no external language model.

Allowed:

- PyTorch modules initialized from scratch;
- the fixed training and validation splits already in the project;
- character, subword, word, set, pointer, pairwise-ranking, and structured-permutation modules trained only from the project training data;
- validation-only model selection.

Not allowed:

- pretrained embeddings or Transformer checkpoints;
- external language-model scoring or reranking;
- external corpora or synthetic labels generated by another language model;
- further inspection or evaluation of the protected test split during architecture development.
